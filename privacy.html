隱私權政策

適用產品：VoxMind - AI語音翻譯與心智圖工作流 ｜ 最後更新日期：2026年9月1日

感謝您使用「VoxMind - AI語音翻譯與心智圖工作流」（以下簡稱「本應用程式」）。本隱私權政策說明本應用程式在您使用錄音、語音轉逐字稿、AI翻譯與摘要、心智圖產生、多格式匯出，以及雲端備份／還原等功能時，如何處理您的資料。使用本應用程式，即表示您已閱讀並同意本政策的內容。

一、本應用程式不設有開發者伺服器

本應用程式採「純前端」架構，沒有任何由開發者自行架設或維運的後端伺服器或資料庫。

本應用程式不會將您的錄音檔、逐字稿、翻譯紀錄或摘要內容，收集、儲存、監控或分析在任何開發者自建的伺服器上。

您的錄音檔案與逐字稿，預設只儲存在您自己的手機裝置本機。

二、第三方AI服務商

本應用程式採「自備金鑰（BYOK）」模式：您需要自行提供 Google Gemini、OpenAI，或 Anthropic Claude 其中一家或多家的 API 金鑰。為了提供語音轉逐字稿、翻譯與摘要功能，本應用程式會將您錄製或匯入的音訊，以及對應的文字內容，透過加密傳輸（HTTPS）直接傳送到您自己選擇並設定的 AI 服務商。

這些資料一旦送出，即受您所選擇之 AI 服務商自身的隱私權政策與服務條款約束，與本應用程式的開發者無關。建議您在使用前，自行參閱您所選服務商的隱私權政策：
  • Google Gemini（Google 隱私權政策）
  • OpenAI（OpenAI 隱私權政策）
  • Anthropic Claude（Anthropic 隱私權政策）

三、雲端備份服務（Google Drive／pCloud）

本應用程式支援將錄音與逐字稿資料備份到您「自己的」Google Drive 或 pCloud 帳號，並可從中還原。

當您使用雲端備份／還原功能時，資料會直接在您的裝置與您自己的雲端帳號之間傳輸，開發者無法存取、也無權存取您的個人雲端空間內容。備份資料會統一存放在您雲端硬碟裡一個固定名稱的資料夾中，方便您自行管理。

四、本應用程式使用的權限

本應用程式僅申請功能運作所必要的權限，並遵循最小權限原則：

  • 麥克風（RECORD_AUDIO）：用於錄製您的語音內容。首次錄音前，本應用程式會先顯示自訂的告知畫面，說明錄音用途與資料流向，待您確認後才會請求系統權限。
  • 網路存取（INTERNET／ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE）：用於呼叫 AI 服務商與雲端備份服務的 API。
  • 前景服務（FOREGROUND_SERVICE／FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE）：用於在錄音過程中維持穩定運作，並於系統通知列顯示錄音進行中的狀態。
  • 通知（POST_NOTIFICATIONS）：用於顯示錄音與處理進度的通知。
  • 藍牙（BLUETOOTH／BLUETOOTH_CONNECT）：用於偵測與連接藍牙耳機麥克風。

本應用程式匯入音檔與匯出 PDF／Word／Draw.io 檔案時，採用 Android 系統的檔案存取框架（Storage Access Framework）與分享機制，不會申請可存取您手機內所有檔案的廣泛儲存權限。

五、資料保存與刪除

您的錄音、逐字稿與相關紀錄，預設僅儲存於您的裝置本機。您可以隨時透過本應用程式內的刪除功能移除個別紀錄，或直接解除安裝本應用程式，即可完整移除本應用程式在您裝置上儲存的所有資料。若您曾使用雲端備份功能，備份檔案會保留在您自己的雲端帳號中，您可自行前往該雲端服務進行刪除。

六、兒童隱私

本應用程式並非針對兒童設計，也不會刻意收集兒童的個人資料。

七、政策異動

若本隱私權政策的內容有所異動，我們會更新本頁面上方的「最後更新日期」。建議您於使用本應用程式前，不定期回來查閱本頁面的最新內容。

Privacy Policy

Applicable Product: VoxMind - AI Voice Translation & Mind Map Workflow | Last Updated: September 1, 2026

Thank you for using "VoxMind - AI Voice Translation & Mind Map Workflow" (hereinafter referred to as "this Application"). This Privacy Policy explains how this Application handles your data when you use features such as audio recording, speech-to-text transcription, AI translation and summarization, mind map generation, multi-format export, and cloud backup/restore. By using this Application, you acknowledge that you have read and agreed to the contents of this policy.

1. No Developer Servers

This Application adopts a "pure front-end" architecture without any developer-built or self-hosted backend servers or databases.

This Application does not collect, store, monitor, or analyze your audio recordings, transcripts, translation logs, or summaries on any developer-owned servers.

By default, your audio files and transcripts are stored solely on your local mobile device.

2. Third-Party AI Service Providers

This Application uses a "Bring Your Own Key (BYOK)" model: you need to provide your own API key(s) for Google Gemini, OpenAI, or Anthropic Claude. To provide speech-to-text, translation, and summarization capabilities, the Application sends your recorded or imported audio and corresponding text content directly to your selected AI provider via encrypted transmission (HTTPS).

Once sent, this data is subject to the privacy policies and terms of service of the chosen AI provider and is independent of this Application's developer. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of your chosen providers before use:
  • Google Gemini (Google Privacy Policy)
  • OpenAI (OpenAI Privacy Policy)
  • Anthropic Claude (Anthropic Privacy Policy)

3. Cloud Backup Services (Google Drive / pCloud)

This Application supports backing up your audio recordings and transcripts to your own Google Drive or pCloud account, as well as restoring from them.

When using cloud backup/restore features, data is transferred directly between your device and your cloud account. The developer cannot and has no authorization to access your personal cloud storage. Backup data is stored in a dedicated, fixed-name folder in your cloud drive for easy personal management.

4. Permissions Used by This Application

This Application requests only the permissions necessary for its functionality, following the principle of least privilege:

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Used to record your audio. Before recording for the first time, the Application displays a custom disclosure explaining the purpose and data flow of the recording, requesting system permission only after your confirmation.
  • Network Access (INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used to call APIs for AI providers and cloud backup services.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE): Used to maintain stable operation during audio recording and display the ongoing recording status in the system notification bar.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to display notifications regarding recording and processing progress.
  • Bluetooth (BLUETOOTH / BLUETOOTH_CONNECT): Used to detect and connect to Bluetooth headset microphones.

When importing audio files or exporting PDF/Word/Draw.io files, this Application utilizes Android's Storage Access Framework (SAF) and native sharing mechanisms without requesting broad storage permissions to access all files on your phone.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

By default, your audio recordings, transcripts, and related records are stored only locally on your device. You can remove individual records at any time using the delete function within the Application, or uninstall the Application to completely remove all data stored on your device. If you have used cloud backup, backup files will remain in your own cloud account and can be deleted directly within that cloud service.

6. Children's Privacy

This Application is not intended for children and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Policy Changes

If the contents of this Privacy Policy change, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically before using the Application.
